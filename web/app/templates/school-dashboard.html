How this is calculated

Best Marks: Shows the best playoff marks for a school in each event.

Avg Marks (individual events only): Shows the average mark for a school in each event where they have at least two athletes with playoff marks. Each athlete's best playoff mark in a season is used in the calculation. For All-Time, each school's all-time best average is ranked against other schools' all-time best averages.

Year / All-Time: Year shows a single season. All-Time combines all seasons.
