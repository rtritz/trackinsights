How this is calculated

Best Marks: best playoff mark per school/event, ranked against statewide playoff best marks.

Avg Marks (individual events only): average of a school's top 2 athletes in that event/year; marks may come from different playoff meets in the same season; requires 2 athletes.

Year / All-Time: Year uses one season. All-Time combines all seasons in scope. In All-Time Best, each athlete/relay counts once (best mark). In All-Time Avg, each school counts once (best top-2 average).
